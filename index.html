Privacy Policy - Astor TuneUp

Last updated: August 5, 2026

Astor TuneUp is a golf swing video analysis app. This Privacy Policy explains how Astor TuneUp handles information when you use the app.

No Account Registration or Login

Astor TuneUp does not require user account registration or login. The app does not allow users to create accounts and does not support account login.

Local Data Storage

Astor TuneUp does not upload your golf swing videos to external servers by default. Videos, audio captured as part of videos, analysis projects, notes, and exported project files are stored locally on the user's device by default.

Astor TuneUp does not upload the user's videos, audio, or analysis projects to Astor TuneUp servers.

Purchases and Subscriptions

Apple App Store payment processing and Google Play Billing are handled through their respective platforms. RevenueCat is used to manage subscription status.

Purchase history may be processed by Apple App Store, Google Play Billing, and RevenueCat for app functionality and subscription entitlement validation. Data transmitted by third-party services is encrypted in transit.

Users can restore previous purchases within the app via the Restore Purchases option.

Deleting Local Data

Users can delete local data by deleting projects in the app, clearing app data, or uninstalling the app.
