Privacy Policy - Astor TuneUp

Last updated: June 30, 2026

Astor TuneUp is a golf swing video analysis app. This Privacy Policy explains how Astor TuneUp handles information when you use the Android app.

No Account Registration or Login

Astor TuneUp does not require user account registration or login. The app does not allow users to create accounts and does not support account login.

Local Data Storage

Videos, audio captured as part of videos, analysis projects, notes, and exported project files are stored locally on the user's device by default.

Astor TuneUp does not upload the user's videos, audio, or analysis projects to Astor TuneUp servers.

Purchases and Subscriptions

Subscriptions and purchase status are processed through Google Play Billing and RevenueCat. Purchase history may be collected by Google Play Billing and RevenueCat for app functionality and analytics.

Data transmitted by third-party services is encrypted in transit.

Deleting Local Data

Users can delete local data by deleting projects in the app, clearing app data, or uninstalling the app.
