Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

1. How do I restore my Pro purchase?

Open the Astor TuneUp Pro page in the app and tap Restore Purchases.

2. Where are my videos stored?

Videos and analysis projects are stored locally on the user's device by default.

3. How do I manage or cancel my subscription?

Subscriptions can be managed or cancelled through your Apple Account subscription settings on iOS, or through Google Play subscription settings on Android.

4. How can I report an issue?

Contact the support email at h104651@hotmail.com with details of the issue you encountered.
